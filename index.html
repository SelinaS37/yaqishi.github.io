About Me

I am a third-year PhD student in the Department of Statistical Sciences at the University of Toronto, supervised by Professor Jesse Gronsbell and Professor Linbo Wang.

I am also a trainee in the CANSSI Ontario STAGE Training Program and a faculty affiliate researcher at the Vector Institute. Previously, I earned an MSc at the University of Toronto and a Bachelor of Mathematics at the University of Waterloo.

Research Interests

My research develops statistical methodology at the intersection of semi-supervised learning, measurement error, and causal inference, with applications to genetics and electronic health record data.

  • Semi-supervised learning
  • Measurement error
  • Causal inference
  • Genetics and electronic health records
