## Research Projects in Progress

### Causal Inference under Differential Measurement Error

Establish identification of causal effects when self-reported exposures are mismeasured in an outcome-dependent manner, developing strategies that leverage multiple instruments under parametric and nonparametric models. Validate finite-sample performance through large-scale simulations in R and analyze UK Biobank data with genetic variants as instruments.

### Heterogeneous Effects of COVID-19 Vaccination on In-Hospital Mortality

Construct an analytic cohort by linking individual-level records across province-wide administrative databases, then analyze more than 100,000 COVID-19 hospitalizations from Ontario population-based administrative data to evaluate whether vaccination-associated mortality reduction differs by schizophrenia status.
