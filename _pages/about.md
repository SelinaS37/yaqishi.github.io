I am a PhD student in the [Department of Statistical Sciences](https://www.statistics.utoronto.ca) at the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca), where I am advised by Professor [Jessica Gronsbell](https://sites.google.com/view/jgronsbell/home) and Professor [Linbo Wang](https://sites.google.com/site/linbowangpku/home). I expect to complete my PhD in June 2028.

I am a trainee in the [CANSSI Ontario STAGE Training Program](https://stage.utoronto.ca). Previously, I earned an MSc in Statistics from the University of Toronto and a Bachelor of Mathematics in Statistics and Actuarial Science from the University of Waterloo.

## Research Interests

My research develops statistical methodology at the intersection of statistical machine learning and causal inference, with applications to genetics, electronic health records, and population-based health data. My current research interests include:

- Statistical machine learning and semi-supervised learning
- Post-prediction inference
- Measurement error and misclassification
- Causal inference, instrumental variables, and Mendelian randomization
- Statistical methodology for large-scale and high-dimensional data

I am always open to exploring new research directions and collaborations. If you are interested in working together, please feel free to reach out via email.
